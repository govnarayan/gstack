# GStack

GStack is an AI skill library for Strategy & Operations, BizOps and Chief of Staff teams. It helps operators turn ambiguous problems into decisions, plans and follow-through.

The library is built for US venture-backed startups and public companies. It covers decisions about growth, capital, people, and how teams work together.

## Walkthrough

See how a scaling AI company turns three team priorities into one company OKR and uses a weekly forecast to act before reliability slips. [Watch the walkthrough and open the templates.](demo/README.md)

## Start with the decision

Share the relevant skill with your AI assistant. Tell it the decision you face, what you know about your company, and what you can decide.
