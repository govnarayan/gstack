# Financial context and authority

Use the library context identifiers. Funding stage, company size, product maturity, business model, scope, public status, and authority are separate. None should be inferred solely from a title or a financial ratio.

| Context | Decision implications |
|---|---|
| `org.stage=seed\|series_a`, small founder-held company | Cash timing, learning runway, committed obligations, concentration, and affordable experiments often dominate. Build only the model needed for the decision. Hands-on financial preparation is allowed when delegated. Missing history requires explicit assumptions rather than invented precision. |
| `org.stage=series_b_c`, expanding organization | Reconcile hiring, pipeline/demand, ramp, unit economics by segment, delivery capacity, and shared costs. Use actual-to-forecast feedback to improve driver estimates. Preserve foundations/service capacity alongside growth investment. |
| `org.stage=series_d_plus\|pre_ipo\|public`, established functions | Work within Finance planning and definitions. Show business-unit and company effects separately, including cross-charges, shared resources, commitments, and governance. Accounting, disclosure, tax, treasury execution, and IR ownership remain outside the operator mandate. |
| `company.product_maturity=pre_pmf\|finding_fit` | Model bounded learning and survival, not an unsupported lifetime value or precise multiyear growth curve. A new product in a mature company can need the same evidence discipline. |
| `company.product_maturity=post_pmf\|scaling_product\|multi_product` | Test repeatability, marginal economics, cohort health, portfolio interactions, cannibalization, and capacity. Scale does not establish continued fit. |
| `org.scope=business_unit\|function\|initiative_or_portfolio` | Use the relevant budget, resources, and decision rights. Distinguish allocated cost, controllable spend, incremental cash, and company-wide effect. Do not optimize local metrics at another team's expense without exposing the trade-off. |

These bands route questions rather than establish automatic thresholds. More detailed planning becomes useful when commitments, working capital, volatility, or cross-team dependence make a simple model materially wrong for the decision. State that failure and the improvement needed. Do not claim that all companies at a given funding stage need the same financial machinery.

## Economic context

Enterprise sales needs contract, implementation, collections, renewal, concentration, and sales-capacity timing. Product-led and consumer models need source/cohort economics, payment/platform costs, refunds, retained use, and support. Marketplace models need completed transaction economics for both sides, incentives, liquidity, and the company's actual revenue share. Platform/ecosystem economics need partner revenue shares, enablement, dependencies, and account ownership. Hybrid models should be separated before consolidation.

For `frontier_ai_lab`, distinguish research investment, uncertain capability, capacity commitment, and product revenue. For `ai_product`, connect successful customer tasks, price, review burden, and cost to serve. For `ai_infrastructure`, test utilization, commitments, deployment, reliability, and customer demand. For `ai_physical_systems`, include hardware/service and working-capital timing. For `b2b_saas`, subscription measures apply only with consistent recurring-revenue and cohort definitions. No provider costs or universal economic benchmark is supplied by this skill.

## Mandate and consequence

A decision owner can select within the actual mandate. A delegated owner can build analysis and perform assigned preparation within limits. Influence without authority needs an accountable sponsor and decision request. A coordinator maintains reconciled inputs, owners, deadlines, and unresolved questions. None automatically authorizes spending, hiring, moving funds, setting pay, signing contracts, financing, or external disclosure.

Use existing policy and consequence to determine review. Small reversible tests need clear spend/time boundaries and a stop condition. Material resource commitments need validated inputs, scenario exposure, accountable Finance/functional owners, and the authorized decider. Public-company or regulated work uses established controls and restricted-data handling. Do not add an unrelated approval process merely because the output contains financial analysis.
