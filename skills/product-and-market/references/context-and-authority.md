# Context and authority variants

The US scope describes the target company. International market, expansion, and operational assignments remain in scope when they fall within that US company mandate. Local legal, tax, employment, and regulatory determinations remain with the accountable specialists.

Stage and headcount are separate signals. Product maturity describes this product, which may differ from the company's stage. Served scope describes the authority and dependencies around this decision, which may differ from company size.

| Context | What changes |
|---|---|
| `org.stage=seed\|series_a`, `org.size_band=under_20\|20_60`, founder-held operation | Favor direct customer evidence, clear hypotheses, affordable probes, and short product briefs. An operator may own hands-on work when delegated. Do not assume demand or repeatability from funding or enthusiasm. |
| `org.stage=series_b_c`, `org.size_band=61_300`, emerging or repeatable operation | Reconcile segments, product outcomes, commercial promises, shared capacity, and service obligations. Use stronger versioning and dependency records where more teams rely on the decision. |
| `org.stage=series_d_plus\|pre_ipo\|public`, larger company or established functions | Fit existing Product and Engineering decision forums. Show portfolio interactions, migration obligations, controls, and business-unit effects. Internal CoS/S&O support does not confer product, legal, disclosure, or functional ownership. |
| `company.product_maturity=pre_pmf\|finding_fit` | Test problem, buyer, willingness to commit, use, and repeat use. Keep options cheap. A roadmap may be a set of learning choices rather than dated output commitments. |
| `company.product_maturity=post_pmf\|scaling_product\|multi_product` | Test whether fit persists by cohort, segment, and product. Include reliability, migration, cannibalization, shared platforms, and operating burden. Existing scale does not validate a new product. |
| `org.scope=business_unit\|function\|initiative_or_portfolio` | Bound outcomes and resources to the served mandate. Name company dependencies and external decision owners. Do not infer company priorities from one unit's incentives. |

The headcount bands above do not establish universal transition thresholds. Add structure when the actual failure is missed commitments, inconsistent decisions, shared dependencies, or loss of evidence. If those failures persist in a small company, explicit records may be justified sooner. If existing systems already solve them, another artifact adds cost.

## Commercial motion

Enterprise products need separate user, champion, budget-holder, procurement, implementation, and renewal evidence. A handful of large deals can distort roadmap priorities. Product-led and consumer products need clear eligible cohorts, time to value, repeat use, and conversion denominators. Marketplace discovery must consider both sides, local or category-specific liquidity, transaction completion, and incentives. Platform and ecosystem work needs developer/partner adoption, dependencies, economics, and the boundary between an integration promise and usable capability. Hybrid motions need separate paths rather than one averaged funnel.

## Archetype

For `ai_product`, examine quality at the real task, review burden, failure recovery, and cost under heavy usage. For `frontier_ai_lab`, research capability, productization, and commercial demand are distinct evidence questions. For `ai_infrastructure`, include availability, integration, performance requirements, utilization, and customer deployment constraints. For `ai_physical_systems`, technical owners supply hardware, safety, field reliability, installation, and service requirements. For `b2b_saas`, recurring-revenue and cohort methods apply only to recurring contracts with consistent definitions.

## Rights and output consequence

A `decision_owner` can make decisions within the stated scope. A `delegated_owner` needs an explicit limit on resources, commitments, and changes. `influence_without_authority` requires a recommendation and accountable sponsors for dependencies. A `coordinator` records and follows through rather than silently deciding product priorities. Confirm who can commit customer-facing dates, product behavior, or resources.

At public companies, use established Product, Finance, Legal, Security, Compliance, and disclosure owners for material obligations and restricted evidence. The operator may prepare analysis and coordinate review. An informal founder-style discussion is insufficient when other teams or customers rely on formal commitments. Conversely, a public-company employer does not justify a company-wide process for a small reversible team decision.

For a reversible experiment, bound spend, audience, duration, and exit conditions. For material or high-impact changes, make exposure, affected groups, downside, independent specialist evidence, and authorized decision explicit. Match review to the real consequence and existing governance rather than adding a universal approval ritual.
