# G&A tools and published prices

Prices checked November 19 and 20, 2026, in US dollars unless marked otherwise. Review February 19, 2027. Starting prices, introductory offers, usage charges, and professional fees vary as shown.

## Finance

| Component | Published terms |
|---|---|
| Books and close | [QuickBooks Online](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/pricing/?m=1) lists Simple Start at $38 and Essentials at $85 monthly before discounts. Its free plan allows two monthly invoices, with an exception for eligible QuickBooks Payments users. [Xero](https://www.xero.com/us/pricing-plans/) lists Early at $27, Growing at $59, and Established at $97 monthly after introductory discounts. Early allows 20 invoices. |
| Financial planning and analysis | [Cube](https://www.cubesoftware.com/pricing) quotes planning, reporting, and scenario tiers. [Float](https://floatapp.com/pricing) lists single- and multi-entity cash forecasts. For companies above £2 million revenue, the US single-entity prices shown were $265 monthly or $215 per month billed annually. |
| Treasury and cash | [Mercury](https://mercury.com/pricing) lists basic banking at $0 monthly. Paid workflow plans start at $35 monthly, waived through December 31, 2026 on the checked page. Treasury lists a $250,000 Mercury balance threshold. Transactions and optional services have separate terms. [Rho](https://www.rho.co/pricing) lists $0 subscription and per-user fees and a 1% foreign currency conversion fee. Treasury management carries a separate fee. |
| Spend and payables | [Ramp Plus](https://ramp.com/pricing/) lists $15 per user per month. [Ramp Bill Pay](https://ramp.com/blog/bill-pay-guides/small-business) describes an additional platform fee based on team size. [BILL](https://www.bill.com/product/pricing) lists Essentials at $49, Team at $65, and Corporate at $89 per user per month. Approver-only seats and transactions have separate terms. |
| Billing and collections | [Stripe Billing](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing) lists 0.7% of billing volume, including transactions processed off Stripe. One-off invoices are excluded from that charge. [Stripe Invoicing](https://stripe.com/invoicing/pricing) lists 0.4% per paid invoice on Starter. Payment processing is separate. [Chargebee](https://www.chargebee.com/pricing/) lists subscription and quote-to-cash plans. Its quoting tool includes the first 50 quotes for Billing customers. Request rates for the intended billing volume separately. |
| Payroll | [Gusto](https://gusto.com/product/pricing) lists Simple at $49 monthly plus $6 per person, Plus at $80 plus $12, and Premium at $180 plus $22. [Justworks Payroll](https://www.justworks.com/pricing) lists $50 monthly plus $8 per employee. Benefits and add-ons have separate terms. |
| Tax | [TaxJar](https://www.taxjar.com/pricing) and its [calculator](https://www.taxjar.com/sales-tax-compliance-savings) list Starter at $39 and Professional at $99 monthly, starting at 200 orders in the calculator. [Anrok](https://www.anrok.com/pricing) lists Starter at $100 per active market monthly and quotes Custom by volume. These charges cover sales-tax software. A certified public accountant's income-tax preparation is a separate engagement. |
| Equity administration | [Carta](https://carta.com/plans/equity-management/) lists Launch at $0. Its headline says up to 25 stakeholders and $1 million raised. Its FAQ says fewer than 25 stakeholders. The [Launch page][launch-plan] says less than $1 million raised. Eligibility at either boundary requires confirmation. Paid plans charge per stakeholder and have annual minimums. A universal rate was unverified. |
| Audit readiness | [FloQast](https://www.floqast.com/pricing) quotes close and finance operations software. An [independent private-company audit](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/article/what-is-a-private-company-audit) is a separate certified public accountant engagement. No audit fee was verified. |
| Insurance | [Embroker](https://www.embroker.com/faq/) quotes premiums after underwriting and describes separate payment processing charges. [Founder Shield](https://foundershield.com/startup-insurance/) quotes coverage. Premiums, coverage limits and deductibles depend on the policy. |

## Legal and company records

| Component | Published terms |
|---|---|
| Entity and board records | [Clerky](https://www.clerky.com/pricing) lists a Company Lifetime Package at $819 and incorporation at $427. California and New York foreign qualification costs $125 plus third-party fees. Other state services use partner prices starting at $160. [Stripe Atlas](https://stripe.com/atlas) lists $500, including Delaware filing and first-year registered agent service. [Delaware](https://corpfiles.delaware.gov/Fee_Schedule/AugustFee2026.pdf) lists incorporation filing fees from $109. Platform and filing charges are separate from counsel fees. |
| Equity plan and 409A | [Eqvista](https://eqvista.com/409a-valuation/) lists annual starting prices of $990, $1,290, $1,990, and $2,590 by complexity. [Carta Launch][launch-plan] names a 409A valuation as a reason to upgrade from Launch. Carta's valuation fee requires a quote. |
| Contracts and signatures | [PandaDoc](https://www.pandadoc.com/pricing/) shows Starter at $19 and Business at $49 per seat per month. Enterprise is quoted. [Docusign](https://ecom.docusign.com/plans/esignature) lists annual commitments of $132 Personal, $360 Standard, and $540 Business Pro. Personal includes five envelopes monthly. Standard and Business Pro include 100 per user annually. [Ironclad](https://ironcladapp.com/pricing/) quotes contract management by customer requirements. Ask which integrations cost extra. |
| Intellectual property | [Cooley GO](https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/form-employee-confidential-information-inventions-assignment-agreement/) publishes a sample employee inventions-assignment form. Counsel fees are separate. The [USPTO](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/trademark-fee-information) lists a $350 base trademark filing fee per class for qualifying Section 1 and 44 applications. Surcharges can apply. |
| Privacy and security | [Termly](https://termly.io/pricing/) lists a $0 privacy plan and paid tiers. [Vanta](https://www.vanta.com/pricing) and [Drata](https://drata.com/plans) quote compliance software. A SOC 2 attestation is a separate auditor engagement. An ISO certification is a separate certification-body engagement. Neither fee was verified. |
| Outside counsel | [Clemenza Law Group](https://clemenzalaw.com/general-counsel) publishes monthly tiers of $1,950, $3,500, $6,000, and $10,000. Deal work may be quoted separately. [Fitter Law](https://fitterlaw.com/pricing/) describes a startup-counsel arrangement with a $2,500 engagement fee and $199 monthly fee. Under its terms, the balance becomes due at financing. |

## People operations

| Component | Published terms |
|---|---|
| Payroll and benefits | [Justworks PEO](https://www.justworks.com/pricing) charges $79 Basic and $124 Plus per employee monthly. [Gusto](https://gusto.com/product/benefits/health/broker-integration) lists outside-broker integration at $6 per eligible employee monthly. Gusto brokerage administration is advertised at no extra charge. Insurance premiums are separate. |
| International hiring | [Deel](https://www.deel.com/pricing/) lists contractor management at $49 and contractor of record at $325 per contractor monthly. Employer of record starts at $599 per employee monthly. [Remote](https://remote.com/pricing) lists contractor management from $29 and employer of record from $599 monthly. Its own-entity payroll starts at $19 per employee monthly. Entity formation and ongoing administration are separate. [UK Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company) lists £100 for online incorporation. |
| 401(k) | [Human Interest](https://humaninterest.com/pricing/) gives an example of $80 monthly plus $5 per employee, or $130 for ten. [Gusto Retirement](https://gusto.com/product/benefits/financial/401k-plans) is the destination from the former Guideline pricing page. A separate Guideline price schedule was unverified. |
| HR information system | [BambooHR](https://www.bamboohr.com/pricing/) lists monthly flat prices through 25 employees: $250 Core, $425 Pro, and $650 Elite. For larger teams, published starting prices are $10.25, $17.50, and $25 per employee monthly. Volume discounts apply. |
| Handbooks | [SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/business-solutions/partner-products/employee-handbooks) and [Blissbook](https://blissbook.com/pricing) offer handbook products. Current prices and plan limits were unverified. |
| State registrations | [Harbor Compliance](https://www.harborcompliance.com/payroll-tax-registration) lists a one-time $600 payroll tax registration package. [Middesk Agent](https://agent.middesk.com/signup) offers registration. Its [pricing help](https://help.middesk.com/en/articles/8246273) did not establish a universal fee. |
