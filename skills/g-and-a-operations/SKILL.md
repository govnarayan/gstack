# Startup finance, legal and people operations

Start from the transaction that has to work. Trace one sale to collected cash, one hire to correct pay, or one grant to an approved ownership record.

Read the company record, contracts, payroll locations, most recent close and delegated authority available to the requester. Check public company and hiring information to identify potential gaps.

State the caller’s role, stage, actual transaction volume, entities, employee locations, obligations and deadline. Distinguish verified facts from assumptions. Ask only for missing inputs that would change ownership or cost.

Use the [finance checklist](templates/finance.md), [legal checklist](templates/legal.md) and [people checklist](templates/people.md) for the affected records. The [provider tables](references/tool-prices.md) include dates, limits and published fees.

## Decide what to own and when

At formation, name one accountable person per record and a backup who can obtain it. A founder may own a result while a bookkeeper or counsel does the work.

At repeatable volume, measure the time from transaction to reconciled record. Add a control where the same error recurs. Record the owner who can resolve exceptions.

When obligations exceed internal review capacity, bring ownership inside before buying another system. Ramp’s lender required an audit. Its first controller rebuilt the accounts and checked the underlying transactions. [Ramp](https://ramp.com/blog/building-ramps-accounting-function/)

At Dropbox, engineers owned billing logic and pricing changes competed for their time. Separate features that differentiate the product from commodity payment processing. [Operator interview](https://journal-entries.anrok.com/p/building-a-subscription-billing-system)

## Worked decision

A synthetic startup has 12 US employees in one state. Its first potential hire abroad would stay for a one-year customer implementation. Compare domestic and international arrangements separately.

At published US rates, Justworks PEO Plus includes medical plan administration and costs $1,488 monthly for 12 employees before premiums. Gusto Simple plus outside-broker integration costs $193 if all 12 are eligible.

The $1,295 monthly difference is the break-even value for premium savings and administration combined. Request like-for-like coverage, employer contributions and service quotes before assigning value to either. [Provider rates](references/tool-prices.md)

A professional employer organization shares employer responsibilities with the company. Check plan coverage at every employee location. Confirm who handles registrations, notices, retirement deductions and service exceptions.

An employer of record employs the overseas hire locally. Contractors sell an independent service. Give counsel the actual work arrangements before pricing either route.

Suppose an owned foreign entity costs $36,000 annually to operate before wages, plus $19 monthly per worker for payroll. An employer of record quotes $599 per worker monthly.

At five workers the entity costs $37,140 annually against $35,940. At six, it costs $37,368 against $43,128. Those figures exclude setup, exit costs, benefits and taxes.

Use the employer of record for this single implementation if counsel confirms suitability. Reprice when country headcount or contract duration changes. An entity requirement can override the cost comparison.

## Connections

A change in employing country changes the hiring start date and loaded cost. People owns the location register. Finance owns the cash forecast and Accounting owns intercompany entries.

When a customer changes billing terms, the billing owner updates due dates and collections. Finance updates projected cash receipts. The approved contract remains the source for both.

For any proposed handoff absent from the connections table, record the source decision, receiving owner and field to change. Counsel, tax advisors and independent auditors resolve specialist questions.
