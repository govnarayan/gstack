# Finance operating checklist

Use one row per component. Record the owner, primary evidence, due date, backup and latest exception. Record the smallest test that proves the process worked.

| Component | Initial check and evidence | Upgrade trigger and scaled check |
|---|---|---|
| Accounting and monthly close | Reconcile every bank and card balance to statements. Sign the close checklist and log late or unresolved entries. [Reinhardt](https://rein.pk/startup-finance-for-founders-part-i-accounting) | When late reconciliations delay the forecast, assign account owners, cutoff dates and review evidence. Test that a second person can reproduce the close. [Ramp](https://ramp.com/blog/cut-monthly-close-time) |
| Financial planning and analysis | Tie starting cash and headcount to the books. Link hiring dates, collections and contractual spending to the cash forecast. [Finance setup](https://mercury.com/blog/setting-foundation-as-first-finance-hire) | When units spend against incompatible assumptions, assign driver owners and reconcile forecasts against one approved version. Distinguish timing misses from changed demand. |
| Treasury and cash | Separate available cash from restricted balances. Test backup access and payment approval before the founder is unavailable. [Founder account](https://rein.pk/startup-finance-for-founders-part-i-accounting) | Add entity-level balances, currency exposure and transfer approvals when funds cannot serve payroll where needed. Test the next payroll with the usual signer absent. |
| Spend and payables | Match vendor, invoice, approval and payment. Confirm changed payment instructions through a previously trusted contact. Keep who requested, approved and paid each invoice. [Internal controls](https://ramp.com/blog/building-ramps-accounting-function/) | When duplicates recur or one person can create and pay vendors, split permissions and reconcile the supplier statement. Test a duplicate and a changed bank account. |
| Billing and collections | Trace one contract to invoice, revenue posting, payment and bank deposit. Record credits, disputed charges and named collection owners. [Segment](https://rein.pk/startup-finance-for-founders-part-i-accounting) | When pricing changes need engineering work, test the old and new plans on actual contract variations before rollout. [Dropbox account](https://journal-entries.anrok.com/p/building-a-subscription-billing-system) |
| Payroll | Reconcile the approved roster, deductions and pay changes to the payroll preview and cash debit. Assign a backup approver. [IRS checklist](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/checklist-for-starting-a-business) | Before the first employee moves jurisdictions, have the provider confirm registration and withholding setup. Reconcile the first run separately. |
| Tax | Keep a calendar of filings, payments, jurisdictions and advisor ownership. Retain submission receipts and exceptions. [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/checklist-for-starting-a-business) | At a new sales channel, country or material activity change, ask the advisor which obligations change. Track income, payroll and sales taxes separately. |
| Equity administration | Reconcile grants and promised awards to approvals, executed documents and the share register. [Gunderson](https://catalyze.gunder.com/en/knowledge-articles/resource/example-pre-seed-due-diligence-checklist) | Before a financing or refresh, reconcile exercised, expired and unissued shares. Have counsel confirm valuation and approval requirements before grants. |
| Audit readiness | Keep source evidence and sign-offs for material balances. Assign an owner to unresolved reconciling items. [Ramp](https://ramp.com/blog/building-ramps-accounting-function/) | When a financing agreement requires audited statements, work back from delivery, opening balances and auditor availability. Software pricing excludes the auditor’s engagement. |
| Insurance | Inventory contractual limits, renewal dates, exclusions, insured entities and deductibles with the broker. [Broker](https://www.embroker.com/faq/) | Before a new product, director, country or customer obligation, ask for a written coverage comparison. Test a plausible claim against its wording. |

Choose tools after testing the failure on a sample transaction. [Provider prices and limits](../references/tool-prices.md)

## Set the monthly close deadline

Work backward from the first report someone outside Finance needs. Ramp’s lender required reporting by day 15. Its controller assigned preparers and reviewers and coordinated late inputs. The team reached day 15, then day 10. Day five was its next target. [Ramp controller’s account](https://ramp.com/blog/cut-monthly-close-time).
