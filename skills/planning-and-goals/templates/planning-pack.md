## F. Pre-approval constraints

Check the plan under a relevant demand surge, dependency delay, scarce-person loss, and a failed strategic assumption. Show which work changes and who decides. Reject double-counted time, fabricated baselines, immature result windows, unaccepted dependencies, and silent target revisions. Keep remaining gaps visible in the approval status.
