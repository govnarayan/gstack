# Financing data room

Create one row per request with its stage, owner, evidence date, approved recipient, link, open exception and due date. Keep a dated record of what each investor received.

1. At pre-seed, attach the charter, amendments, bylaws and approvals. Reconcile founder shares and vesting to signed records. Flag missing evidence for counsel. [Gunderson](https://catalyze.gunder.com/en/knowledge-articles/resource/example-pre-seed-due-diligence-checklist)
2. Show issued shares, promised grants, available options, outstanding financing agreements and investor side letters. Reconcile the ownership schedule before quoting dilution. [Cooley](https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/sample-vc-due-diligence-request-list/)
3. Trace ownership of the product to signed assignments from each founder, employee and contractor. Ask counsel about earlier employers, licenses and missing signatures. [Gunderson](https://catalyze.gunder.com/en/knowledge-articles/resource/example-pre-seed-due-diligence-checklist)
4. Attach the dated pitch, demonstration and cash plan. Mark projections and assumptions. Identify the evidence the round will buy and the minimum cash balance during a delay. [Timing](https://www.madrona.com/when-to-raise/)
5. At seed and Series A, add monthly accounts and cash statements. Reconcile claimed recurring revenue to customer contracts, invoices and collections. Explain cancellations, one-time sales and concentration. [Worked cohort review](../../operating-cadence/examples/company-metrics-review.md) [Request list](https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/sample-vc-due-diligence-request-list/)
6. At expansion, show retention by customer start date and segment. Trace an unusual cohort back to its contracts. Reconcile each recurring expense excluded from reported margins to the accounts. [Worked cohort review](../../operating-cadence/examples/company-metrics-review.md)
7. Provide debt instruments, liens, material customer and supplier contracts, outstanding claims and regulatory correspondence to approved reviewers. Ask counsel which consents change the closing date. [Cooley](https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/sample-vc-due-diligence-request-list/)
8. At later private stages, add subsidiary records, tax and audit materials, employment obligations, and prior board approvals where applicable. Explain forecast misses using the [review pack](../../operating-cadence/templates/review-pack.md). [Diligence categories](https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/sample-vc-due-diligence-request-list/)
9. Record each missing document as absent, pending or inapplicable with a reason. Give reviewers the approved version and revoke access when their review ends. [Recordkeeping](https://www.cooleygo.com/you-cant-afford-to-be-disorganized/)

## Choosing access controls

Use the smallest plan that passes a test with a separate external account. Try opening a restricted folder, downloading a protected file and using the link after revocation.

US published prices checked November 19, 2026. Review February 19, 2027 or before purchase. Annual commitments, room administrators and external reviewers affect the comparison differently.

## Worked exception

A synthetic subscription company reports $120,000 in recurring annual revenue from one account. Its signed agreement includes a $20,000 installation charge.

The finance owner moves that charge to one-time revenue and records $100,000 recurring. Update the pitch, cohort table and forecast from the same contract before releasing the new version.
