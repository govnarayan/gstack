# Sales operations

Start from the buyer action the forecast assumes. Read the current pipeline, contracts, quota plan, territory rules and last forecast as it stood before close.

State the company’s selling motion, buyer, cycle, rep capacity and requester’s authority. Research public product and pricing evidence. Ask for the missing records that could change the decision.

## Run the sales record

1. Define stage exits using buyer evidence. A demo booked shows access. A named budget owner, agreed next action and dated buying process support a stronger claim. [GitLab stages](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/commercial/comm-sales-opp-stages/)
2. Record opportunity owner, amount, product, buying group, target date, last buyer event, next event and its date. Log a reason when a date or stage moves. [Commercial handbook](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/commercial/comm-sales-opp-stages/)
3. Route accounts using a stable account identifier, parent relationship and territory. Name who owns exceptions so account reassignment does not create duplicate pipeline. [GitLab territories](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/field-operations/gtm-resources/)
4. Balance territories using reachable accounts, past demand, service needs and seller capacity. Count open opportunities and ramped rep-months. Equal account counts can leave unequal economic opportunity. [Iterable case](https://www.fullcast.com/case-studies/how-iterable-is-planning-equitable-territories-for-growth-with-fullcast/)
5. Set quota against selling time, cycle, attainable opportunity and conversion evidence. Before a repeatable motion emerges, review how reps spend their day and what advances buyers. [Datahug account](https://review.firstround.com/the-company-changing-magic-of-sales-operations-done-right/)
6. Define the commissionable event, eligible amount, split ownership, payment timing, cancellations and exceptions. Run the proposed plan against a discounted annual deal, an expansion and an early cancellation. [First plan](https://www.saastr.com/dear-saastr-how-should-i-build-our-first-sales-comp-plan/)
7. Before sending a nonstandard quote, obtain approvals for discount, terms and channel treatment. Compare rep payout, net contribution and expected collections under both prices. [GitLab deal desk](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/field-operations/sales-operations/deal-desk/)
8. At the forecast call, inspect the change from last week. Ask which customer event supports the new date, what remains outside the rep’s control and when it must happen.
9. Preserve the forecast snapshot and reconcile it with wins, losses, slips and changes in amount. Recalibrate by segment and stage only where there are comparable past deals.

When buying behavior is still changing, one observed customer commitment can tell the founder more than a weighted total built from unrelated deals.

Once enough comparable opportunities close each cycle, score the prior forecast on its own terms. Separate date error from value error before changing probabilities.

## Worked forecast call

A synthetic enterprise seller calls $240,000 for the quarter. Deal A is $140,000 and has a confirmed procurement meeting before quarter end.

Deal B is $100,000. The rep moved its close date twice. The customer has yet to identify a budget approver. The manager removes B from the committed category.

Keep B open with the next buyer action and owner. The revised commit is $140,000, subject to procurement. Finance models timing separately from the total possible pipeline.

On A, a proposed 10% discount lowers the sale to $126,000. At an illustrative 8% of net bookings, commission falls from $11,200 to $10,080.

Change the commission basis, revenue forecast and expected cash receipt together. The weekly sweep flags the slip and the pending pricing decision. The owner checks the eventual signed amount.

When an amendment changes how charges ramp or adds seats to an existing charge, get the deal desk’s ruling before you send the quote. GitLab’s public guide gives conflicting instructions for those cases. For a subscription replacement, verify the account mapping on the new subscription before cancelling the original. [GitLab Deal Desk](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/field-operations/sales-operations/deal-desk/).
