# Channel and enablement choices

Use this reference after identifying the commercial constraint. These are optional methods to evaluate and prepare work with the owning functions. They are not a required campaign program.

## Customer and field requirements

Commercial readiness includes more than promotion. Maintain the relationship between customer needs, field evidence, product behavior, policy, technical capability, deployment, support, and the claims Sales can make. A recurring security requirement might need a product capability, a policy decision, stronger documentation, an approved exception, or a decision not to serve the use case.

Record the exact buyer requirement, affected segment and buying stage, independent account recurrence, supporting conversation, economic exposure, available workaround, responsible expert, and next decision. Test whether a requirement truly blocks a deal or is one of several reasons not to buy. Pipeline exposure is not guaranteed incremental revenue. Compare product, policy, enablement, service, and commercial responses before assigning a roadmap request.

Readiness evidence should show what is available, to whom, under what terms/restrictions, with which deployment and service requirements. Name what can be communicated now, what needs specialist review, and what remains uncommitted. Close the loop with the field teams that raised the need. Product, Security, Policy, Legal, Finance, and other experts retain their decision authority.

## Content, search, and distribution assets

Distinguish searchable material that answers existing questions from shareable material that creates attention. Choose based on where the target buyer seeks information and whether the company can produce credible material within its time/resource constraint.

Map customer questions, objections, alternatives, and implementation needs to buyer stage. Compare topic options on customer relevance, product connection, distinctive evidence, realistic distribution, effort, maintenance, and a meaningful next step. An editorial theme or search-volume gap is not a reason to invest by itself. Derive content from customer language and evidence rather than copying a competitor's topic list.

For comparison pages, distinguish switching intent, broad alternatives, direct head-to-head evaluation, and comparisons between other products. Maintain dated competitor facts once and reuse them consistently. Include where each alternative fits, meaningful limits, total price for a stated use case, migration effort, and relevant proof. Do not fabricate defects or self-award every comparison.

For free tools or downloadable resources, compare qualified downstream demand against build, promotion, service, abuse, maintenance, and update costs. Evaluate ungated, partly gated, and gated alternatives. An email address is not the same as a buyer. A calculator must distinguish prospect-supplied inputs, modeled value, and verified outcomes. A tool built only for attention can consume the same engineering capacity needed for core product work.

For scaled search pages, check actual demand distribution, unique useful content per page, data provenance/refresh, and conversion intent. Technical specialists assess crawlability, indexing, rendering, structured data, URL migration, and accessibility. A static fetch that misses rendered data is insufficient to declare a feature or schema absent. Do not import fixed click-depth, keyword-density, or linking quotas as acceptance criteria.

AI-search presence is an uncertain distribution hypothesis. Evaluate relevant buyer queries, observed citations, traffic and qualified outcomes across repeated comparable checks. Verify platform mechanisms, crawler rules, and claimed improvements against current primary sources before operational use. Training access, search access, and citation are not interchangeable decisions. Do not change crawler access merely because a marketing source claims it guarantees citations.

App-store discovery requires the actual listing, visual assets, target audience, search/conversion evidence, and product experience. A prominent brand's choices may be deliberate, but scale alone does not prove they are correct. Platform specifications, ranking mechanisms, and eligibility need current official verification. The operator can assess commercial opportunity and requirements while specialist teams implement listing and technical changes.

## Paid, outbound, lifecycle, and community

For paid acquisition, separate audience, offer, creative, landing experience, conversion, retained use, and attribution. A cheap click can be a poor customer. Compare cost and quality by actual cohort and incremental spend. Platform learning periods, creative minimums, format limits, bidding rules, and automatic budget increments are specialist inputs to verify. Authorization to analyze an account does not authorize spending or campaign changes.

For outbound, connect a real observation about the buyer to a plausible problem, evidenced value, and a small clear ask. Personalization that does not change relevance is decoration. Separate drafting from sending. Follow-ups should add relevant information and respect explicit stop conditions. Do not disguise promotional messages as replies or invent prior contact, warm introductions, or proof.

For lifecycle messages, specify the audience state, entry event, message purpose, next useful action, exit/suppression conditions, owner, and overlapping contact. Behavior and sales-cycle timing should govern messages rather than a copied day-by-day sequence. Verify delivery of promised content and measure downstream adoption, retention, and contribution. Opens or clicks alone do not establish business value.

For community, state the value members gain, why they return or help others, staff/moderation capacity, escalation, and connection to commercial outcomes. Compare joining an existing community with creating one. Membership count and posting frequency can obscure unanswered questions, passive value, and a small group doing all the work. Avoid treating source participation ratios as universal laws.

Referral and affiliate programs have different trust, incentive, and operating mechanisms. Identify trigger, sharing behavior, referred-user activation, reward event, economics, verification, fraud, partner conflict, and repeated use. Model incremental retained customers after incentives and administrative cost. Do not apply a general average referral improvement to the company's own program.

## Enablement and release communication

Choose a deck, one-pager, objection reference, demo, proposal, case study, or buyer card based on who needs it and the decision it supports. Capture objections in the words field teams hear, the likely concern, an accurate response, evidence, and a follow-up question. Unknown causes remain hypotheses. A blocker may be legitimate rather than an objection to overcome.

Use customer-specific workflows in demonstrations, and verify that shown behavior is available under the proposed offer. Value calculators should expose adoption, time saved, realized cash/capacity benefit, implementation, and ongoing costs. Time saved does not automatically eliminate headcount cost. Legal and commercial owners supply binding terms. The operator can prepare a decision-ready proposal without inventing contract authority.

Scale announcement effort to affected audience, meaningful change, and readiness. Compare company-owned distribution, paid access, and third-party audiences against this product's buyers and readiness. A general-availability statement needs verified availability, terms, service readiness, enablement, and ownership. A successful announcement can still precede weak activation or poor economics.

## Evidence tests

Every draft should retain the distinction between verified proof, modeled benefit, customer statement, and hypothesis. Review copy for clarity, audience relevance, proof, accurate terms, next step, and preservation of the author's intent. Simulated expert panels can critique a draft but cannot certify buyer acceptance, scientific validity, or publish readiness.

For split tests, define the question, assignment unit, meaningful effect, primary business outcome, guardrails, sample/duration rationale, and analysis rule. Do not interpret a p-value as the probability that a result is random or true. A test can compare a coherent bundle when the question is about the bundle. Isolating individual causes then requires a different design. Specialist statistical review is warranted when that distinction changes a consequential decision.
