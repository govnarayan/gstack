# Turn shared distribution into incremental growth

Use this procedure when a business could grow through a parent's, sibling unit's or shared platform's customer access. The decision is whether a specific offer and selling motion produce retained incremental value without damaging another business or overwhelming delivery. Access to an account list is only the first dependency.

The strongest alternative is preserving the unit's direct motion. Shared selling can divert sellers from their own targets, displace an existing offer and add approvals without creating customer value. Start with that comparator. This is original operating guidance and the completed case below is synthetic.

## Establish what can actually be offered

Use [commercial operating interfaces](../../cross-functional-execution/references/commercial-operating-interfaces.md) to resolve account, compensation and delivery rights. Use [partnership operation](partnership-operating-model.md) for reciprocal activation. This procedure joins those decisions to a group-level investment test.

Build one reconciled population, retaining source IDs and exclusion reasons. Resolve duplicates and corporate-family relationships first. Then inspect customer need, product overlap, usable capability, permission to share information/contact the customer, current opportunity ownership, buying timing and delivery eligibility. Keep uncertain records outside committed reach until their owners resolve them. Apply the same eligibility rules to treatment and comparison populations.

For overlapping products, compare complement, replacement, bundle and no joint offer at the customer-task level. Identify the existing offer's contribution at risk and the Product/commercial owner who decides positioning. Do not suppress a useful unit offer merely because a parent has a similarly named product. Equally, do not count a transfer between group products as wholly new growth.

The operator prepares and reconciles the list, tests the handoff, calculates economics and executes delegated activation work. Account owners approve contact. Product owns supported scope. Sales and Finance approve the credit/payout rule, with other actual required approvers. Delivery owners accept capacity. A sponsor's enthusiasm does not supply these rights.

## Make the first transaction executable

Before broader exposure, trace an eligible account through introduction, receiving-owner acceptance, scope, approved proposal, booking, usable onboarding and retained use. Identify the exact system record and accepting owner at every handoff. Replay a duplicate account, rejected lead and disputed credit as well as the happy path.

Set the selling exchange explicitly: relationship ownership, specialist time, response commitment, claims permitted now, booked event, quota credit, cash payout, cancellation treatment and dispute owner. Quota credit and cash payout have separate effects. Internal credit does not become group revenue or a second cash cost. Include incentive cost only once in contribution.

Reserve qualified implementation/support hours after existing service and other commitments. Bound invitations and accepted implementations separately. An invitation cap cannot prevent overcommitment unless the system also stops accepting work beyond available delivery. Preserve the unit's authority over local product experiments within accepted obligations. Escalate conflicts over parent resources through their actual owner, with a reduced-scope option the unit can execute.

## Completed synthetic decision

A software unit considers a 90-day shared-selling pilot. Its 1,200 source records become 1,000 unique customer groups after 200 duplicate records are reconciled. Sequential exclusions remove 100 unsupported use cases and 150 overlapping offers without an approved customer proposition. They then remove 150 without confirmed contact/data permission and 200 already in the unit's active pipeline. The remaining 400 groups meet a fixed eligibility definition. Exclusion order is retained so accounts cannot be subtracted twice.

Within comparable segments, 200 groups are randomly assigned the joint offer and 200 retain the existing motion. Assignment is by corporate customer group to reduce cross-arm contact. The 90-day retained outcome is paid use of the supported task through day 90, including the originally assigned population whether contacted or not. Unresolved outcomes remain unresolved. Ordinary sales continues in both arms and conflicting campaigns are logged. These design choices limit some rival explanations but do not guarantee a precise result from a small pilot.

| Evidence or cost | Offer arm | Existing-motion arm |
|---|---:|---:|
| Assigned eligible groups | 200 | 200 |
| Accepted qualified opportunities | 40 | 25 |
| Paid implementations | 24 | 12 |
| Retained paid task use at day 90 | 18 | 10 |

Assume the complete synthetic observation has no pending cases. The observed retained difference is eight accounts. Contribution per retained account over the same 90 days is $900 after ordinary variable delivery cost. Additional program costs are $100 per booked implementation ($2,400), $1,600 setup, $800 selling administration and $1,000 extra specialist support not included in ordinary delivery. A separate ledger records $500 of lost contribution on a displaced group offer.

`group incremental net contribution = (18 − 10) × $900 − $2,400 − $1,600 − $800 − $1,000 − $500 = $900`

The unit's 18 attributed retained accounts imply $16,200 contribution before program costs, but that is not the incremental group result. Transfers or internal allocation changes cannot rescue the $900 case. This calculation is an observed difference under invented inputs, not a claim about a real intervention or statistical certainty.

The delivery owner has 72 qualified hours after existing obligations. Initial triage consumes 20 hours and implementation takes two hours per accepted paid implementation. Observed demand is `20 + 24 × 2 = 68 hours`. The upper scenario of 32 implementations requires 84 hours and fails. The accepted-work cap is therefore 26 on this arithmetic, subject to peak timing and specialist coverage. Sales cannot promise 32 because the average case fits.

The sponsor authorizes only the bounded pilot after the functional approvals above. The operator configures the approved routing and acceptance cap with the system owner, runs one permitted end-to-end handoff and checks rejection handling. No new product approval is added to the unit's existing local experiment process. The ongoing commercial owner takes the next cohort after inspecting actual accepted work, retained use and credit reconciliation.

## Decide what the evidence permits next

The first case breaks even at seven retained incremental accounts because $6,300 / $900 = 7. At eight, another $900 of uncounted cost or displaced contribution removes the margin. A repeat cohort with identical outcomes and no additional setup would yield $2,500, but that remains a forecast until comparable buyers, rights and capacity are available. At recurring cost of $4,700, six incremental retained accounts are needed. Re-estimate payout cost if booking volume changes.

Recommend a second bounded cohort only if the owners accept the remaining uncertainty and delivery cap. The strongest objection is that eight additional retained accounts could be sampling variation or an unobserved campaign difference. Inspect segment outcomes, contamination and uncertainty before broad spending. If a credible comparison is unavailable, report attributed retention and a bounded incrementality range, then show whether the investment still works at its lower end.

Preserve the original eligibility, assignments and baseline alongside current forecasts. Use one initiative ID in the commercial system, leadership review and [benefit register](../../financial-resource-decisions/references/value-realization.md). Expand, narrow, redesign or stop from retained customer value, incremental group economics, service quality and local decision speed. An enabled field team or larger pipeline alone does not close this decision.
