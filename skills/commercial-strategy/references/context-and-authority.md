# Commercial context and authority

## Stage, size, and product maturity

At `seed` or `series_a` with `under_20` or `20_60` employees, distinguish founder-led learning from a repeatable commercial system. Favor direct buyer access, short evidence records, simple offers, bounded experiments, and explicit delegated ownership. Avoid adding channels faster than the team can learn from them. Funding is not proof of product-market fit.

At `series_b_c` or `61_300` employees, test whether a motion scales beyond its original buyers and founders. Reconcile commercial targets, pipeline quality, product constraints, delivery capacity, handoffs, and unit economics. Differentiate a temporary execution failure from a motion that cannot work for the next segment.

At `series_d_plus`, `pre_ipo`, or `public`, and at larger size bands, use the established commercial, Finance, Product, and control owners. Evaluate channel conflict, portfolio cannibalization, shared customers, policy, multi-quarter commitments, and restricted information. A business-unit recommendation must show local economics and company effects separately.

For `pre_pmf` or `finding_fit`, growth work should test whether the chosen buyer values and repeatedly uses the offer at viable economics. More acquisition may buy useful learning, but name that purpose and budget. For `post_pmf` or `scaling_product`, verify repeatability by cohort and marginal spend. For `multi_product`, show cross-sell, cannibalization, common distribution, and product-specific economics.

There is no fixed revenue or employee threshold for adopting a CRM policy, separate commercial team, or pricing committee. The choice changes when repeated ownership disputes, exception volume, handoff failures, or data reconciliation make the current mechanism more costly than a clearer one. Quantify that volume and cost where possible.

## Motion variants

| `company.go_to_market_motion` | Decision emphasis |
|---|---|
| `enterprise_sales` | Buying committee, proof, deployment, procurement, sales cycle, rep ramp, services, renewal, concentrated-account exposure. A few deals do not support a precise conversion estimate. |
| `product_led` | Eligible signups, real activation, self-serve purchase, paid conversion, usage costs, support, expansion, and the point where sales assistance adds economic value. |
| `consumer` | Acquisition incrementality, user cohorts, repeat use, net receipts after platform/payment shares, churn, refund/chargeback, and clear subscription terms. |
| `marketplace` | Both sides, liquidity in the relevant market, transaction completion, incentive cost, fraud, retained transaction share, and off-platform alternatives. |
| `platform_or_ecosystem` | Developer/partner value, activation, integration and enablement, partner economics, channel dependence, and control of customer relationship. |
| `hybrid` | Separate paths, segments, economics, and handoffs before producing a combined view. |
| `unknown` | Establish the actual buying and use paths. Do not select a SaaS funnel by default. |

## Archetype and authority

For AI businesses, cost and customer value can vary with usage, task success, human review, latency, reliability, and deployment. Use current measured company inputs. Do not insert provider prices or universal inference-margin targets. Lab capability does not establish a saleable product. Infrastructure and physical-system offers may need technical validation, capacity, installation, safety, and field service before commercial scale.

A `decision_owner` selects within its mandate. A `delegated_owner` may perform specified research, drafting, tests, or execution within the agreed limits. `influence_without_authority` requires commercial sponsors and an explicit decision request. A `coordinator` maintains shared definitions, evidence, owners, and follow-through. None of these titles automatically authorizes external outreach, campaign spending, contract changes, pricing publication, or disclosure.

At public companies, do not assume ownership of staffed Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, Finance, Legal, HR, accounting, or Investor Relations. Small-company delegation can include hands-on work, while specialist questions still require the right expertise. Use current company authority and policy rather than importing the source author's organization chart.
