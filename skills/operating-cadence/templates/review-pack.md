## C. Weekly business review

Headline observation and why it matters now:

Sweeps run before the meeting (see [standing sweeps](../references/reviews-and-health.md#build-the-pack-from-standing-sweeps)): status changes, number moves, blocked work, dropped handoffs, decisions due. Each item names its owner and its decider from the company's own record, such as a RACI, RAPID, DACI or DRI list. Items with no owner are listed as exceptions.

Prior actions: completed with evidence, overdue, canceled with authority/reason, or pending.

Explain mutually exclusive categories and the evidence for coverage. Test peak periods, specialist absence and incident load. Record whether reserves are still available. Compare a staffing floor with rotation, narrower/staged rollout, postponement and qualified temporary coverage. Treat effectiveness as unproven until observed under relevant load.

## G. Operating record after the review

| ID | Decision/action/investigation | Evidence/options/dissent | Actual approver or owner | Due/effective date | Resources/work displaced | Completion evidence | Effect check/revisit | Communicated to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | | | |

Amendment record: original version and result, triggering evidence, authorized replacement, new version, affected dependencies, forecast and budget implications, and recipients. A change in forecast is not a change in approved commitment or spend authorization.

Usefulness check: preparation effort, attendee time, signal freshness, decisions delayed, actions reopened, missing sources and uncertainty lost. Review a real execution, then keep, combine, redesign or retire the forum with evidence.
