# How operating decisions connect

Follow the change in a company record. A strategic choice funds a goal. The goal needs an owner and decision rights. Weekly evidence changes the forecast and the next plan.

For example, follow price through commissionable bookings, the revenue forecast, cash runway, financing timing and funded hiring. A secondary sale changes ownership without adding operating cash.
