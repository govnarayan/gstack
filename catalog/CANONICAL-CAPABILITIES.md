# GStack capabilities

Each capability describes work an operator needs to perform. Related capabilities sit together in a skill, with company context determining how the guidance applies.

The [package registry](skill-packages.csv) connects these capabilities to the skill folders. [Foundation](../GSTACK-FOUNDATION.md) defines the context identifiers.

| ID | Capability | What it covers | Context and limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| `company-context` | Company context and operating diagnosis | Establish company stage, size, business model, scope, maturity, mandate, authority, and the actual problem before choosing a method. | `org.*`, `company.archetype`, `role.*`, `operating.maturity`. Broad. |
| `strategy-choices` | Strategy and strategic choices | Frame the challenge, choices, where to play/how to win, strategic beliefs, exclusions, and implications. | `work.domain=strategy`. Distinguish company, business-unit, function, and product strategy. |
| `strategy-review` | Strategy review and assumption testing | Revisit whether the strategy is still right, separately from whether execution is on track. Surface changed evidence and decision implications. | `work.domain=strategy`. Broad. Cadence adapts to uncertainty and governance. |
| `business-model` | Business model and value creation | Trace customer value to revenue, costs, incentives, and durable economics. Identify assumptions that must hold. | `work.domain=business_model`. `company.archetype` required when models differ. |
| `long-range-plan` | Long-range direction and strategic narrative | Translate strategy into a small set of longer-horizon outcomes, capabilities, and boundaries. | `work.domain=planning`. Horizon and artifact adapt by stage, product maturity, and archetype. |
| `annual-plan` | Annual plan and resource envelope | Convert longer-term direction into annual bets, sequencing, resources, and assumptions. | `work.domain=planning\|resource_allocation`. Usually post-product-market-fit, with lighter pre-scale variants. |
| `goals-and-okrs` | Goals, OKRs, and commitments | Decide whether formal goals help. Select outcomes, owners, measures, commitments, and alignment approach. | `work.domain=planning`. Do not assume OKRs fit pre-PMF or every team. |
| `portfolio-priorities` | Priorities and portfolio choices | Compare work by strategic contribution, urgency, cost, dependencies, and opportunity cost. Make explicit what waits. | `work.domain=resource_allocation`. Company, business-unit, or portfolio scope. |
| `operating-cadence` | Operating cadence and business reviews | Set the minimum useful rhythm for strategy, planning, execution, performance, and decisions. | `work.domain=planning\|performance_and_metrics`. Cadence adapts to operating maturity, volatility, and AI-enabled information flow. |
| `performance-information` | Company metrics, forecasts, and analysis | Define company measures by business mechanism, execute equal-tenure cohort and mix analysis, reconcile revenue/contribution/cash, forecast outcomes, and diagnose decision-relevant variance. | `work.domain=performance_and_metrics\|decision_support`. Adapt by archetype, commercial motion, product maturity, and served scope. Detailed functional scorecards remain specialist extensions. |
| `run-change-separation` | Run-the-business and change-the-business distinction | Keep stable service/health obligations visible while evaluating change work and launches. | `work.domain=planning\|cross_functional_delivery`. Especially useful when shared capacity is constrained. |
| `cross-functional-delivery` | Cross-functional initiative delivery | Clarify outcome, accountable owner, decision rights, dependencies, capacity, escalation, and evidence of completion. | `work.domain=cross_functional_delivery`. `role.authority` required. Scope can be company, function, or initiative. |
| `decision-rights` | Decision rights and decision records | Make the decision owner, delegated authority, consultation, reversibility, deadline, and rationale clear. | `role.authority` and `governance.regime`. Adapt to public-company and regulated requirements. |
| `program-management` | Program management | Choose a program approach and tools. Set up charter, sponsorship, benefits, schedule and critical path, capacity, budget, RAID, decision and change logs, acceptance, transition and closure. Choose RAPID, RACI or lighter decision-rights arrangements by situation. | `role.authority` required, with `work.domain=cross_functional_delivery`. Structure scales with interdependence, consequence and existing systems. |
| `executive-leverage` | Chief of Staff and executive leverage | Increase leadership capacity through synthesis, follow-through, issue routing, preparation, and translation between intent and company action. | `role.mandate=strategy_and_operations\|business_operations\|chief_of_staff\|special_projects`. `role.authority` controls what the operator may decide. |
| `executive-communications` | Executive communication and decision documents | Produce concise decision memos, strategy narratives, internal updates, and leadership communications grounded in evidence and audience needs. | `work.domain=executive_communications`. Adapt to audience and governance. Document formats are adapters. |
| `executive-governance-support` | Executive and board governance support | Help internal executives prepare operating or strategy material for board and governance forums when the role is assigned to support it. | `governance.regime=venture_private\|public_company`. Does not own Investor Relations or corporate disclosure. |
| `organization-design` | Organization design and change | Match responsibilities, interfaces, leadership capacity, and change approach to the strategy and company stage. | `work.domain=organization`. `org.size_band`, `org.scope`, `operating.maturity` matter. |
| `operating-model-design` | Company operating model design | Diagnose value flows and competing causes, compare operating models, design current, interim and target arrangements across decision rights, structure, processes, interfaces, systems, measures and incentives, secure agreement, implement and maintain. | `work.domain=organization\|strategy`. Distinguishes the operating model from the org chart and meeting cadence. |
| `people-operations` | Leadership, hiring, and people operations | Define roles, hiring needs, onboarding, management practices, and talent decisions in support of the operating model. | `work.domain=organization`. Employment-law and HR compliance remain specialist-reviewed. |
| `customer-problem` | Customer and problem discovery | Identify user/customer segments, jobs, pain, evidence quality, and unmet need before committing product or GTM resources. | `work.domain=product_and_market`. `company.archetype` and customer type shape methods. |
| `market-competitive` | Market, customer, and competitive understanding | Size and segment opportunities, understand alternatives and competitors, identify market structure, and state uncertainty. | `work.domain=product_and_market\|strategy`. Market definitions and evidence must be explicit. |
| `product-direction` | Product direction and roadmap choices | Translate customer and company strategy into product outcomes, sequence, roadmap choices, and explicit non-goals. | `work.domain=product_and_market`. Technical and product-domain extensions attach by context. |
| `product-delivery` | Product delivery artifacts and execution records | Create or assess briefs, requirements, specifications, stories, launch records, and handoffs that make product work executable. | `work.domain=product_and_market\|cross_functional_delivery`. Engineering methods remain technical extensions. |
| `commercial-growth` | Commercial strategy and operating analysis | Execute positioning, channel, pipeline, conversion, retention, and subscription-cohort analysis with Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success owners where those roles exist. In a small startup, support or directly own work that leadership explicitly delegates. | `work.domain=product_and_market\|business_model`. Adapt by `company.go_to_market_motion`. Does not assume functional ownership. |
| `pricing-monetization` | Pricing and monetization | Design and evaluate price, packaging, monetization, and incentive structures using customer and economic evidence. | `work.domain=business_model`. Archetype and buyer model required. Legal/tax review may apply. |
| `financial-resource` | Financial analysis and resource allocation | Model financial outcomes, runway, scenarios, budgets, unit economics, headcount, and resource trade-offs. Work with Finance where staffed, or perform delegated analysis in a small startup. | `work.domain=finance\|resource_allocation`. Public/private reporting context matters. Does not assume controller, accounting, or Investor Relations ownership. |
| `ai-technical-fluency` | AI and technical operating fluency | Reconcile AI service economics and usable capacity, evaluate research and release dependencies, and compare constrained technical investments with the responsible owners. | US AI companies. Adapt by `company.archetype`, product maturity, and company size. Technical assessment is a context extension. |
| `ai-augmented-operator` | AI-augmented operator work | Select company processes, build bounded automation contracts and recovery tests, operate exceptions, and measure quality and net capacity after review and maintenance effort. | `role.mandate=strategy_and_operations\|business_operations\|chief_of_staff`. Adapt by task risk, data access, and authority. |
| `risk-governance` | Risk, controls, security, and governance coordination | Identify material risks, controls, escalation paths, and accountable functions. Help internal operators surface and route issues. | `work.domain=risk_and_controls`. Adapt by US public-company or venture context. Legal, Compliance, Security, and technical owners retain their mandates. |
| `scenario-response` | Scenario planning and response | Explore plausible outcomes, triggers, choices, and actions under uncertainty or time pressure. | `work.domain=strategy\|decision_support`. Crisis and regulated contexts require specialist input. |
| `facilitation` | Leadership facilitation and meeting preparation | Prepare and facilitate a meeting around a clear decision or work outcome, then record owners and follow-through. | `role.mandate` and `role.authority`. Adapt to remote, cross-functional, or board settings. |
| `research-analysis` | Research and decision support | Frame a question, gather suitable evidence, distinguish fact from inference, synthesize alternatives, and identify what would change the decision. | `work.domain=decision_support`. Evidence method and source access are explicit. |
| `self-serve-analytics` | Self-serve analytics | Answer business questions from internal data with AI-assisted SQL and code: inspect schemas and grain, define metrics, join and deduplicate correctly, handle NULLs and dates, analyze cohorts, reconcile results, and build maintained decision dashboards within access, cost and data-handling rules. | `work.domain=analytics\|performance_and_metrics\|decision_support`. Does not own data infrastructure or certify reported financial figures. |
| `fundraising-and-capital` | Fundraising and capital decisions | Decide when to raise and compare financing structures, prepare investor diligence, and hand off the private board decision and follow-through. | Finance and resource allocation for US venture-backed private companies. Founders, authorized directors, Finance, and counsel retain their decision and approval rights. |
| `talent-and-hiring` | Talent and hiring | Set hiring standards for specific work, design interviews, compare role scope and pay, and evaluate coaching, retention and role changes. | `work.domain=organization`. Adapt by size, stage, maturity and authority. People and legal specialists retain their mandates. |
| `g-and-a-operations` | Startup finance, legal and people operations | Establish owners and controls for finance, legal and people records. Compare provider costs, coverage and handoffs. | Private startups. Adapt by stage, size, maturity and authority. Counsel, tax advisors and independent auditors retain their mandates. |
| `workplace-problem-solving` | Workplace problem solving | Select the angles relevant to an operating problem, test competing explanations, and choose a decision, pilot, or next piece of evidence. | `work.domain=decision_support`. Adapt by scope, authority, and consequence. |

## Validate a library change

Run from the library root with Python installed:

```sh
python -m pip install -r requirements-dev.txt
python scripts/validate_library.py
```
