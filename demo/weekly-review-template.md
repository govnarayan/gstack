# Weekly CEO review | Growth and capacity

Sales, Product, Finance and Engineering update one shared forecast by the day before the existing meeting.

| From | Bring this |
| --- | --- |
| Sales | Customer go-live dates and the peak usage they are expected to add. Flag changes and distinguish confirmed starts from tentative ones. |
| Product and Finance | Rollout dates, affected customers and the estimated increase in peak usage. Explain how you estimated the increase. Keep the assumptions visible. |
| Engineering | Actual peak load and failures. The load the platform has passed at the quality target. The next capacity milestone and test date. |
| Strategy & Operations | Last week's forecast versus actual. A combined forecast for the next peak. Any decision needed before the next review. |

Spend the meeting on forecast errors, upcoming capacity conflicts and decisions. Compare the expected peak with tested capacity and the agreed room for forecast error. Keep normal operations and incident response moving between meetings.
