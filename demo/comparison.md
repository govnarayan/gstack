# What the comparison showed

Two agents using the same model received the same fictional company case. One could use GStack. The other received the case alone. Each prepared a plan before seeing the same week-three update.

Both created a shared company OKR and a forward-looking review. Both anticipated the capacity shortfall and recommended staging the rollout, moving flexible work overnight and reassigning an engineer. GStack also built an explicit reserve into the plan. Its week-three review checked whether the revised forecast of 9,500 jobs left enough room below the tested limit of 10,000.

The walkthrough focuses on the capacity and reliability results. The comparison measured the content of two plans and their responses to a fictional update.
