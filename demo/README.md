# GStack walkthrough

[Watch the 90-second presentation with narration](gstack-walkthrough.mp4). A [captioned cut](gstack-captioned.mp4), [slide deck](slides.pdf), and [timed transcript](transcript.md) are also available.

A fictional AI company has more demand than its platform can comfortably serve. The demo shows GStack turning three team priorities into one company OKR and setting up a weekly review that catches a capacity shortfall before reliability slips.
