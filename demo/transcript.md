# GStack walkthrough transcript

## 0:00 | What GStack does

GStack helps operators make better decisions with AI. Use it when you’re setting up a process, weighing an investment, or thinking through a strategy.

## 0:09 | Give it context

Give it what you’d tell a teammate. What’s the job? Where’s the company today? Who makes the call, and when? That changes the advice.

## 0:17 | The library

That context is the foundation. GStack draws on seventeen areas of expertise. Here we’ll use planning to set the goal and the weekly review to drive it.

## 0:27 | Three teams, three plans

Imagine you’re the ops lead at an AI company that hit a hundred million dollars in annualized revenue in under a year. Sales, Product, and Engineering each have a plan. What happens when they all land in the same week?

## 0:40 | One company goal

Give all three teams one goal: serve more customers without slowing them down. Can the platform handle more jobs at peak? And do fewer of them fail?

## 0:50 | Look ahead

Last week we expected eight thousand jobs at peak, and that’s what we got. Next week’s customer starts bring us to ten thousand. The rollout could add twenty percent. That takes us past what Engineering has tested.

## 1:03 | Make the call

The CEO acts before the new load arrives. Product stages the rollout. Sales and Product launch an incentive to run flexible batches overnight. Engineering moves someone onto the capacity upgrade. Another feature waits a week. Next week, check what actually shifted.

## 1:20 | Close

GStack brings operator experience, research, and frameworks into the conversation. It helps you see the trade-off and choose what fits your company. You make the call.
