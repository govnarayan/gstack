# Company OKR | Make room for growth

**Objective.** Serve more customers without slowing them down.

| Key result | What counts |
| --- | --- |
| Expand tested capacity | Prove the platform can handle 15,000 jobs in an hour with at most 0.5% failures by quarter-end. The current verified limit is 10,000. Test a mix that reflects the planned customer starts and feature usage. |
| Improve live reliability | Cut production peak-hour failures from 1% to 0.5% or less over the final four weeks of the quarter. |

## Agree on the plan before committing dates

| Team | Commitment |
| --- | --- |
| Sales | Rank the next customer starts. Bring dates and expected peak usage for each. Confirm what fits before promising a date. |
| Product and Finance | Agree rollout timing and estimate peak usage for each stage. Make the assumptions visible and revise them after launches. |
| Engineering | Commit the people and test date for the upgrade. Set the room to leave below tested capacity for forecast error. |
| Strategy & Operations and CEO | Reconcile the plans on one calendar. The CEO approves the order of launches and go-lives, the staffing, and what moves if the forecast worsens. |

In the weekly meeting, compare last week's expected peak with actual load and failures. Then combine confirmed starts and upcoming rollouts into one forecast. If the next peak leaves too little room, change the plan before launching or promising more starts. Keep capacity at the last verified level until the upgrade passes its test.
