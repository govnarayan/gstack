# Business framework library

Start with the decision the business needs to make. Select a method because its mechanism answers that question, then produce its output. This library contains 43 methods across six domains. It does not claim to cover every useful framework.

The strongest objection to a framework library is that it can replace investigation with familiar labels. Use these cards only after stating the problem, evidence, constraints, and actual decision owner. A missing input remains unknown. A framework does not supply evidence or authority.

## Select and apply

1. State the business question, feasible alternatives, consequence, and decision date. Read the [Foundation context](../GSTACK-FOUNDATION.md) and record the fields that change the answer.
2. Use the routes below to choose one primary method. Add another only when it answers a different unresolved question. Read the selected card in its reference file, not the whole library.
3. Collect the specified evidence, carry out the procedure, and deliver the named output. Show the rejected alternative, strongest counterargument, uncertainty, and condition that would change the decision.

`Supports` uses existing capability IDs from [skill packages](../catalog/skill-packages.csv). `Context` uses Foundation identifiers. An identifier without a value means collect its value and adapt the method. No new company taxonomy is introduced.

## Context changes the application

- `role.authority`: A coordinator or influence-only operator prepares the choice and gets the decision owner's mandate. A method cannot turn consultation into approval authority.
- `org.scope`: Define company, unit, function, or initiative boundaries before comparing markets, allocating costs, centralizing work, or selecting a portfolio method.
- `company.product_maturity` and `company.go_to_market_motion`: Pre-fit work needs learning evidence. Mature-product work can use historical cohorts and economics. Sales, consumer, marketplace, and platform motions need different units and time windows.
- `org.size_band` and `operating.maturity`: Add explicit roles or forums when actual dependencies, repeated delays, and consequences justify them. No headcount band automatically requires a matrix, management layer, or committee.
- `governance.regime`, `data.sensitivity`, and `task.consequence`: Respect existing delegated authority, data handling, and control requirements when testing or implementing a choice.

## Boundaries and provenance

The cards are independent prose summaries of public methods with instructions for applying them and worked examples using made-up data. They do not reproduce commercial canvases, diagrams, worksheets, firm templates, paid courses, scoring weights, or company implementations. Source links identify the author or teaching source. Practitioner documentation is labeled as such rather than credited as invention.

The source supports a method's mechanism, not a guarantee that it works for this company. Selection, failure conditions, and example decisions are this library's judgments. Named methods and trademarks remain their owners'. Use the linked official sources for licensed templates and full treatments.

This initial selection does not cover every strategy school, detailed M&A execution, experimental statistics, specialized financial valuation, jurisdiction-specific governance, or all industry operating systems. Extend it when a business problem cannot be answered adequately by the current methods, with a public source and a substantive card rather than a new name alone.

[Three worked examples](examples/three-decisions.md) show how to choose a method, use it and decide what would change the call.

## Use the case on the selected card

Identify the fact that would change your choice before opening the case. Compare the buyer, operating constraint, available authority and cost of reversal with your situation. Read the original operator account when a difference matters.

Where a company supplies the example and another author supplies the method, the card applies the method to that case. A company's reported outcome alone cannot show that using the method caused it.
