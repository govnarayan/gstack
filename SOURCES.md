# Authorship, sources and credits

## Author and operating perspective

GStack draws first on [Govind Narayan’s operating experience](https://www.linkedin.com/in/govindnarayan/), including building a company, serving as COO through an exit, and leading corporate development. The guidance starts with decisions operators face, the constraints they work under, and who carries the decision through.

Published frameworks, research and accounts from other operators help test assumptions, check what a method is meant to do, and show where it may apply. The skill references explain how each source informs the work.

That operating perspective spans three connected areas:

1. Strategy and economics: business models, growth choices, resource allocation, and build/buy/partner decisions.
2. Execution and organization: operating models, priorities, capacity, decision rights, and leading work across functions.
3. Change under real constraints: scaling, crisis, acquisitions, integrations, and exit, where incentives, authority, and scarce resources determine what a plan can deliver.

## Trace a recommendation to its use

The [knowledge index](catalog/knowledge-index.md) identifies the question, source, access, reading depth and reference that uses it. A linked operator case establishes what that team observed under its conditions. Where a reference applies a method to a case, the proposed application belongs to this library.

For example, an acquisition announcement establishes the terms the parties disclosed. A closing release establishes whether it closed. A subsequent impairment addresses the value of an asset. None supplies the return on the partnership the buyer could have pursued instead.
